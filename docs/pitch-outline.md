# Paytm Build for India AI Hackathon Pitch Outline
### Track 3: Autonomous AI Teammates — AwaazLedger

## Slide 1: The Problem — The Dreaded "Paise Kat Gaye Par Pahuche Nahi"
- Over 14 Billion UPI transactions happen monthly in India.
- 0.8% to 1.5% encounter technical declines, timeouts, or hanging debits.
- That's 100M+ monthly instances of panic, embarrassing merchant arguments, and multi-day customer support back-and-forth.

## Slide 2: The Solution — AwaazLedger
- An Autonomous AI Teammate for Paytm that doesn't just chat—it heals payments.
- Voice-first (Sarvam AI for vernacular Bharat).
- 3-Way Autonomous Reconciliation (CBS ↔ NPCI UDIR ↔ Paytm).
- Instant Bridge Credit for high-trust users up to ₹2,000.

## Slide 3: Autonomous Architecture & Safety
- Deterministic State Machine + RBI Policy Engine.
- Instant relief for small tickets; automated escalation for high risk.
- Zero hallucination: Policy thresholds are hardcoded in compliance-governed modules.

## Slide 4: Business Impact for Paytm
- **90% reduction** in UPI dispute support ticket volume.
- **< 5 seconds** mean time to user relief (vs 48 hours industry average).
- Retention & Trust: Users never abandon Paytm at merchant counters.
- Automated recovery: Bridge credit reclaimed via automated NPCI reversal switch.

## Slide 5: Roadmap & Future Expansion
